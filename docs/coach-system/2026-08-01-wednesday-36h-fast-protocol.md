## Latest Dylan instruction

Dylan does not like the half-fast structure.

Wednesday should become a full-day fast, scheduled as a 36-hour fast.

This supersedes prior Wednesday 900-cal / half-fast language in older docs and settings.

## Working schedule

Default 36-hour structure:

- Tuesday: finish dinner by ~7:30-8:00 PM.
- Wednesday: full fast day, no calories.
- Thursday: break fast around ~7:30-8:00 AM, ideally after morning weigh-in and before/after training depending on the day's session logistics.

Approximate duration: Tuesday evening to Thursday morning = ~36 hours.

If training quality or GI response makes Thursday morning awkward, the break-fast timing can be adjusted, but the governing intent is now: Wednesday is a true no-calorie fast day.

## What counts during the fast

Allowed:

- water
- black coffee
- unsweetened tea
- zero-calorie electrolytes
- plain salt/electrolyte water
- doctor-prescribed non-caloric meds/supplements as normally directed

Not allowed as part of the fast:

- Nurri
- whey
- yogurt
- cream/milk
- caloric drinks
- snacks
- “just protein” meals

## Electrolyte coaching

Dylan needs electrolyte guidance because he does not yet know the routine.

Principle:

A 36-hour fast is mostly a hydration/electrolyte management problem, not a willpower contest.

### Simple default protocol

Use Dylan's existing Re-Lyte or equivalent zero-cal electrolyte product if available.

Wednesday:

1. Morning
   - 16-24 oz water on waking.
   - 1 serving electrolytes or a salty water equivalent.
   - Black coffee is okay.

2. Midday
   - 16-24 oz water.
   - 1 serving electrolytes if headache, lightheadedness, unusual fatigue, or heavy sweating.

3. Late afternoon / early evening
   - 16-24 oz water.
   - 1 serving electrolytes if symptoms or if total sodium has been low.

4. Evening
   - Water to thirst.
   - Avoid over-drinking plain water without electrolytes.

### Practical sodium target

For a full-day fast, a reasonable practical target is roughly 2-4g sodium across the day from electrolyte mix/salt, adjusted for sweat, activity, blood pressure/medical context, and symptoms.

Do not megadose. Do not chug salt water. Spread intake.

### Symptom guide

Likely electrolyte/hydration signals:

- headache
- lightheadedness on standing
- unusual fatigue
- muscle cramps
- “wired but weak” feeling

Response:

- pause
- drink water with electrolytes/salt
- sit if lightheaded
- reassess in 15-30 minutes

Stop/modify the fast if:

- fainting
- chest pain
- severe dizziness
- confusion
- vomiting
- symptoms do not improve with hydration/electrolytes

## Training implications

Wednesday is currently mobility + arms. That remains the best training day for a full fast because it avoids heavy lower/anchor work.

Rules:

- Keep Wednesday session light/moderate.
- No failure training.
- No HIIT.
- Optional cardio only if energy is good.
- If lightheaded, skip cardio and keep the fast safe.

Thursday after the fast:

- Break fast with protein-forward, GI-friendly food.
- Do not break with a huge greasy meal.
- If training Thursday morning before breaking fast, Coach should watch performance and symptoms closely.

## Break-fast guidance

Default break-fast:

- protein first
- moderate carbs
- low/moderate fat
- easy-on-GI foods

Examples:

- whey/Nurri first, then normal breakfast/lunch later
- Greek yogurt + whey/fruit if tolerated
- lean protein + rice/potato later

Avoid:

- giant high-fat meal immediately
- heavy restaurant food as first intake
- trying to “make up” all missed calories immediately
