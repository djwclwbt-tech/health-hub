# Egg White Grill, batch of 6

==One sandwich is about 415 calories and 39 g protein. With a Nurri and a Honeycrisp, breakfast is about 685 calories and 70 g protein.== The batch takes about an hour on Sunday. Each morning takes 3 minutes.

Six covers the week: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday. Wednesday is your fast.

!!! warning "Check these two labels first"
    **The egg carton.** The receipt only says "MM LIQ EGG". Member's Mark egg whites fit that line, but make sure the carton says egg whites. Whole liquid egg would about double the calories and add about 10 g of fat per sandwich.

    **The thigh package.** Thigh labels vary a lot. If yours is far from about 140 calories per 3.5 oz raw, tell me and I'll redo the numbers.

The real Chick-fil-A sandwich is 300 calories, 27 g protein, 8 g fat and 29 g carbs. Yours has 12 g more protein and 115 more calories. The extra comes from a bigger egg portion, thigh instead of breast, and a full slice of cheese.

To get closer to the original, use half a slice of cheese (saves 40 calories and 3 g fat). If chicken breasts are still in your freezer, swap them in (saves about 30 calories and 4 g fat, adds about 3 g protein).

## Shopping check

You bought everything for the sandwich today. **Nurri isn't on this receipt.** Make sure you have 6 at home. Check you also have 6 apples.

## Ingredients for 6

- 18 oz raw chicken thighs, trimmed (about 5 thighs, 1 lb 2 oz)
- 3 cups liquid egg whites (1½ of the 16 oz cartons)
- 6 English muffins
- 6 slices provolone

**Marinade**

- 3 tbsp orange juice, or 2 tbsp more lemon juice with 1 tbsp water
- 1 tbsp lemon or lime juice
- 1 tbsp white vinegar
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp each paprika, garlic powder and onion powder
- ½ tsp black pepper

The citrus and salt are what make it taste like Chick-fil-A. Their ingredient list shows orange, lemon and lime juice, vinegar, salt, onion, garlic and paprika. The marinade adds only a few calories per sandwich.

## Steps

1. **Marinate.** Trim the big pieces of fat off the thighs. Pound them to an even ½ inch. Mix the marinade, add the chicken, and leave it in the fridge for at least 30 minutes.
2. **Bake the whites.** Heat the oven to 325°F. Line a 9×13 pan with parchment and spray it with olive oil. Pour in the whites and add a pinch of salt and pepper. Bake about 20 minutes, until the middle no longer jiggles. Don't let it brown.
3. **Cook the chicken.** Air fry at 380°F for 12 to 14 minutes, flipping halfway. Work in batches and don't crowd the basket. It's done at 175°F if you have a thermometer, or when the thickest piece has no pink. Rest 5 minutes, then slice flat across the grain.
4. **Cut and divide.** Cut the whites into 6 squares. Split the chicken into 6 equal piles, about 2 oz cooked each.
5. **Build the kits.** On each square of parchment, stack whites, chicken, then cheese. Blot the whites with a paper towel first. Fold the parchment closed. Don't add the muffin yet.
6. **Store.** Put 3 kits in the fridge for Monday, Tuesday and Thursday. Put 3 in a freezer bag for Friday, Saturday and Sunday. Keep the muffins in their bag.

**Why the muffin stays out:** a muffin stored with egg and chicken turns soggy. Toasted fresh, it tastes like the real thing.

## Each morning

1. Toast a muffin, about 2 minutes.
2. Unwrap a kit onto a plate and cover it with a damp paper towel.
3. Microwave 60 to 75 seconds, until steaming. From frozen, use half power for 2 minutes, then full power for 30 seconds. Or move a frozen kit to the fridge the night before and heat it as usual.
4. Stack it on the muffin. Have it with your Nurri and apple.

## Fitting it into your day

On a 2,000 calorie, 200 g protein day, this breakfast leaves **1,315 calories and 130 g protein**. That's the same calories-to-protein ratio as your whole day, so it doesn't make the rest harder.

Your older plan kept starch to dinner on weekdays, and this muffin breaks that rule. It's fine if your calories still fit. On days dinner runs high, drop the rice or potato at dinner, not this breakfast.

Log it in Cronometer as a custom food so it's one tap each morning: 415 calories, 39 g protein, 30 g carbs, 13 g fat.
