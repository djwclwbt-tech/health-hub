# Egg White Grill, batch of 6

==One sandwich is about 455 calories and 58 g protein. With a Nurri and a Honeycrisp, breakfast is about 725 calories and 88 g protein.== One oven, one temperature, about 10 minutes of work and 45 minutes of baking. Each morning takes 3 minutes.

Six covers the week: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday. Wednesday is your fast.

!!! warning "Check the frozen chicken label first"
    This recipe assumes plain raw breasts. If the bag says fully cooked or breaded, tell me first, because the steps and numbers change.

## Ingredients for 6

- **Eggs:** 4½ cups liquid egg whites (2¼ cartons), 1½ cups Fage 0%, 1 tsp each kosher salt, garlic powder and onion powder, plenty of black pepper
- **Chicken:** 1½ lb frozen raw breast, 1 tsp each kosher salt, paprika and garlic powder, ½ tsp black pepper
- **To finish:** 6 English muffins, 3 slices provolone cut in half

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Line a 9×13 pan with parchment and spray it. Line a sheet pan with foil.
2. Whisk the Fage and seasoning into the whites until there are no lumps. Pour into the 9×13.
3. Put the frozen breasts on the sheet pan, spray them, and season. Bake both pans.
4. Pull the eggs at about 30 minutes, when the middle no longer jiggles.
5. Pull the chicken at about 45 minutes. Thick pieces may need longer. It must reach 165°F, or have no pink in the thickest piece. Rest 5 minutes, then slice flat.
6. Cut the eggs into 6 squares and split the chicken into 6 equal piles. Stack eggs, chicken, then half a slice of cheese on squares of parchment and fold closed.
7. Put 3 kits in the fridge for Monday, Tuesday and Thursday. Put 3 in the freezer for Friday, Saturday and Sunday. Keep the muffins in their bag.

## Each morning

1. Toast a muffin, about 2 minutes.
2. Unwrap a kit onto a plate and cover it with a damp paper towel.
3. Microwave 60 to 75 seconds, until steaming. From frozen, use half power for 2 minutes, then full power for 30 seconds.
4. Stack it on the muffin. Add pickles, mustard or hot sauce if you want more flavor. Have it with your Nurri and apple.

## Leftovers

- **Fresh thighs:** this recipe doesn't use them. Cook or freeze both packs within 2 days.
- **Egg whites:** keep unopened cartons sealed. Use the open carton by the date on it.
