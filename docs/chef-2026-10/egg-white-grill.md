# Egg White Grill, batch of 6

==One sandwich is about 470 calories and 58 g protein. With a Nurri and a Honeycrisp, breakfast is about 740 calories and 88 g protein.== Everything cooks tonight in about an hour. Each morning takes 3 minutes.

Six covers the week: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday. Wednesday is your fast.

!!! warning "Check these two labels first"
    **The egg carton.** The receipt only says "MM LIQ EGG". Member's Mark egg whites fit that line, but make sure the carton says egg whites. Whole liquid egg would add about 160 calories and 17 g of fat per sandwich.

    **The frozen chicken.** This recipe assumes plain raw breasts. If the bag says fully cooked or breaded, tell me first, because the steps and numbers change.

**Where the calories went.** Chicken, egg whites and Fage all cost about 5 calories per gram of protein, so those got bigger. Cheese costs about 16 and the muffin about 30, so the cheese got cut in half and the sauce carries the flavor.

**To cut further:**

- Skip the cheese: saves 40 calories and 2.5 g protein.
- Eat it open-faced on half a muffin: saves 75 calories and 2.5 g protein.

## Shopping check

**Nurri isn't on this receipt.** Make sure you have 6 at home. Check you also have 6 apples and at least 1½ cups of Fage.

## Ingredients for 6

- 1½ lb frozen raw chicken breast
- 4½ cups liquid egg whites (2¼ of the 16 oz cartons)
- 6 English muffins
- 3 slices provolone, cut in half

**Chicken marinade**

- 3 tbsp orange juice, or 2 tbsp more lemon juice with 1 tbsp water
- 1 tbsp lemon or lime juice
- 1 tbsp white vinegar
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp each paprika, garlic powder and onion powder
- ½ tsp black pepper

**Egg whites**

- ½ tsp each garlic powder and onion powder
- Pinch of salt and plenty of black pepper

**Fage sauce**

- 1½ cups Fage 0%
- 2 tbsp yellow mustard
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp smoked or regular paprika
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- Pinch of salt, and hot sauce if you like it

Pickle slices and hot sauce add a lot of flavor for almost no calories. Add them in the morning if you have them.

## Steps

1. **Thaw.** Seal the frozen chicken in a bag and put it in a bowl of cold water for 30 to 45 minutes. Change the water once. Don't use hot water.
2. **Marinate.** Slice thick breasts in half through the middle so they're about ½ inch thick. Mix the marinade and add the chicken. Leave it while you do the next two steps.
3. **Make the sauce.** Stir everything together in a jar and put it in the fridge. It keeps all week.
4. **Bake the whites.** Heat the oven to 325°F. Line a 9×13 pan with parchment and spray it with olive oil. Stir the seasoning into the whites and pour them in. Bake about 25 minutes, until the middle no longer jiggles. Don't let it brown.
5. **Cook the chicken while the whites bake.** Air fry at 380°F for 10 to 12 minutes, flipping halfway. Work in batches and don't crowd the basket. It's done at 165°F, or when the thickest piece has no pink. Rest 5 minutes, then slice flat across the grain.
6. **Cut and divide.** Cut the whites into 6 squares and fold each one to fit the muffin. Split the chicken into 6 equal piles, about 3 oz cooked each.
7. **Build the kits.** On each square of parchment, stack whites, chicken, then half a slice of cheese. Blot the whites with a paper towel first. Fold the parchment closed. Keep the muffin and sauce out.
8. **Store.** Put 3 kits in the fridge for Monday, Tuesday and Thursday. Put 3 in a freezer bag for Friday, Saturday and Sunday. Keep the muffins in their bag.

**Why the muffin and sauce stay out:** a stored muffin turns soggy, and yogurt can split when you microwave it. Add both fresh.

**Why you stop at 165°F:** breast has little fat, so a few degrees past done is what makes it dry. A thermometer is the best fix if you have one.

## Each morning

1. Toast a muffin, about 2 minutes.
2. Unwrap a kit onto a plate and cover it with a damp paper towel.
3. Microwave 60 to 75 seconds, until steaming. From frozen, use half power for 2 minutes, then full power for 30 seconds. Or move a frozen kit to the fridge the night before and heat it as usual.
4. Spread ¼ cup of sauce on the muffin and stack it. Have it with your Nurri and apple.

## Fitting it into your day

On a 2,000 calorie, 200 g protein day, this breakfast leaves **1,260 calories and 112 g protein**. That's plenty of room per gram of protein, so the rest of the day gets easier.

Your older plan kept starch to dinner on weekdays, and this muffin breaks that rule. It's fine if your calories still fit. On days dinner runs high, drop the rice or potato at dinner, not this breakfast.

Log it in Cronometer as a custom food so it's one tap each morning: 470 calories, 58 g protein, 35 g carbs, 7 g fat.

## Leftovers

- **Fresh thighs:** this recipe doesn't use them now. Cook or freeze both packs within 2 days.
- **Egg whites:** keep unopened cartons sealed. Use the open carton by the date on it.
- **Fage sauce:** any extra is good on chicken at lunch.
