# Egg White Grill, batch of 8

==One sandwich is about 420 calories and 49 g protein. With a Nurri and a Honeycrisp, breakfast is about 690 calories and 79 g protein.== Only the eggs need the oven. The chicken is already cooked. Each morning takes 3 minutes.

Eight covers Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday, plus next Monday and Tuesday. Wednesday is your fast.

Carbs: 35 g in the sandwich and about 68 g in the whole breakfast. The chicken uses the Member's Mark label: 110 calories, 19 g protein, 2 g fat and 3 g carbs per 3 oz. The other numbers are estimates from typical labels. The real Chick-fil-A sandwich is 300 calories and 27 g protein.

## Ingredients for 8

- **Eggs:** 2½ cartons liquid egg whites (40 oz by weight, about 4½ cups). Shake first, 1½ cups Fage 0%, 1 tsp each kosher salt, garlic powder and onion powder, plenty of black pepper
- **Chicken:** 28 oz Member's Mark fully cooked mesquite chicken fillets, frozen. No seasoning needed.
- **To finish:** 8 English muffins, 4 slices provolone cut in half

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 350°F on plain Bake, not Convection Bake. Rub the big deep black NutriChef pan with 1 tsp of oil, corners included.
2. Whisk the Fage and seasoning into the whites until there are no lumps. Pour into the pan.
3. Bake on the top rack. That dark pan runs hot, so check at 25 minutes. Pull the eggs when the middle no longer jiggles, about 30 minutes. Brown patches and cracks are normal. Cool 5 minutes.
4. While the eggs bake, weigh 28 oz of frozen chicken. Tare the empty bowl first. Microwave the chicken on a plate for about 2 minutes to thaw it, then warm it on the bottom rack for 15 to 20 minutes, until hot in the center. Cut each of the 4 breasts in half, about 3.5 oz each, then slice flat.
5. Cut the eggs into 8 squares. Build whole sandwiches on the muffins: half a slice of cheese, egg, then chicken. Let everything cool fully, then zip each one in its own bag.
6. Put 3 in the fridge for Monday, Tuesday and Thursday. Put 5 in the freezer for Friday, Saturday, Sunday and next Monday and Tuesday.

## Each morning

1. Take the sandwich out of its bag. Never put the bag in the air fryer.
2. From the fridge, air fry at 325°F for 5 to 6 minutes. From frozen, air fry at 300°F for 12 to 14 minutes, or move it to the fridge the night before and heat it as usual.
3. It's ready when the center is hot and the cheese has melted. Add pickles, mustard or hot sauce if you want more flavor. Have it with your Nurri and apple.

## Fitting it into your day

On a 2,000 calorie, 200 g protein day, this breakfast leaves **1,310 calories and 121 g protein**.

**Keep the Nurri.** At 5 calories per gram of protein, it's about as lean as the chicken. If breakfast feels too big, have it at 3 PM instead.

Here's a sample day, using Monday's salmon dinner. Numbers are estimates.

Your older plan kept starch to dinner on weekdays, and this muffin breaks that rule. On days dinner runs high, drop the rice or potato at dinner, not this breakfast.

## Leftovers

- **Fresh thighs:** this recipe doesn't use them. Cook or freeze both packs within 2 days.
- **Egg whites:** keep unopened cartons sealed. Use the open carton by the date on it.
- **Mesquite chicken:** put the rest of the bag back in the freezer right away.
